About this property

A contemporary four-bedroom + 1, all with closets and windows, four bathrooms, villa on a 753 m² plot — the largest of the eight in the quiet Poça dos Moinhos development at Nadadouro — minutes from Foz do Arelho, the Óbidos Lagoon and the Silver Coast beaches, and about an hour from Lisbon airport.

Built in 2011 across three floors, the house opens onto a 60 m² living and dining room with full-height glazing and an open staircase, flowing straight out to the terrace, lawn and private pool. The 28 m² kitchen is fully fitted with Teka appliances and has its own dining area. Four bedrooms — the largest at almost 29 m² — and three bathrooms are finished in Saloni ceramic tile, wood-effect flooring and Roca sanitaryware.

The lower floor adds a 65 m² garage, a 34 m² storage room and a dressing room, bringing the total to 454 m² of gross construction area.

The villa is rated Energy Class B: double brick walls with Wallmate insulation, thermal-break aluminium frames with double glazing and exterior shading, an insulated pitched roof, Samsung split air conditioning throughout, and a 300-litre Baxi solar thermal system with gas backup for hot water.

The 629 m² garden is walled and entirely private, laid to lawn around a south-west-facing pool and sun terrace — sun on the water from late morning through to sunset.

Offered turnkey. Available to purchase or to let long-term.
