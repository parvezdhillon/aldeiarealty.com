About this property

​Detached villa with pool, guest annex, and established garden — Salir de Matos, Caldas da Rainha

​Ten minutes from Caldas da Rainha, where the only thing in front of you is farmland and an orchard. This detached two-level villa sits in the hamlet of Trabalhias on 1,388 m² of land, a 756 m² urban plot plus its own 632 m² rustic land, with 440 m² of gross construction and 220 m² of private living area. Licensed for use in 1999 and finished in light render over a stone-clad base.

​Main floor. Open-plan living and dining room, fitted kitchen with island and gas hob, three bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a glazed sunroom that catches the afternoon light. Balconies facing east and west, morning coffee on one side, sunset on the other.

​Lower level (220 m²). Garage and workshop, laundry, generous storage, a media room, and a self-contained apartment of roughly 80 m², making the house ideal as a multi-generational home or offering the possibility of additional income.

​Grounds. A 5.3 × 8 m swimming pool with a sun terrace, a separate annex full of possibilities, mature gardens, and plenty of fruit trees.

​Comfort and efficiency. Pellet-stove heating, electric hot water, double glazing throughout, and solar panels on the roof. Energy certificate class C, valid until 2035.

​Óbidos is 15 minutes away, Foz do Arelho beach and the Óbidos Lagoon are 15–20 minutes away, and Lisbon is about an hour via the A8. Viewings by appointment.
